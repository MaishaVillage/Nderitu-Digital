For over two decades I have worked at the intersection of creativity and communications — helping organizations tell stories that matter and professionals build influence that lasts.

My journey began in the craft disciplines — graphic design, photography, and videography — where I developed a deep understanding of how visual storytelling moves people. Through Pinkfoot Consult Limited, I built a creative agency rooted in design excellence, and through Photomagic Studio Limited, I delivered high-quality audiovisual services to some of Kenya's leading corporate clients.

Now I am in the middle of a deliberate evolution. I am stepping fully into digital media strategy and thought leadership — bringing two decades of creative execution to a bigger mission: helping organizations and professionals lead in the digital-first era with clarity, authority, and purpose.

My work sits at the crossroads of content, communications, and community. I believe that in today's world, your story is your strategy — and I exist to help you tell it at scale.
